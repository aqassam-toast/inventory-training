Getting started & access

Access, setup, and help getting started.

How do I get access to Toast IQ Inventory?

Contact your Toast sales representative to check availability and get set up.

Related guide: What to expect during onboarding →

Where is Toast IQ Inventory available?

Open Beta is available in the UK & Ireland, Canada, and Australia, and for purchase in select U.S. districts.

Is this available outside the U.S.?

Yes. Toast IQ Inventory is available in the UK & Ireland, Canada, and Australia. Accounting integration availability is limited to the U.S. and Canada.

Does Toast IQ Inventory cost extra?

Yes. It is a paid addition to your Toast subscription. Check Toast Shop or ask your Toast representative for pricing.

Do I need the Toast Retail module?

No. You do not need to purchase Toast Retail to use Toast IQ Inventory.

Why can’t I see my menu items?

In Categories, select the menus you want to include. If items still do not appear, contact the Inventory team.

Related guide: Mastering the item catalog →

How do I share feedback?

Use the feedback form for suggestions, or email the Inventory team for help.

Build your catalog

Items, recipes & ingredients

Create items, connect recipes, and keep your catalog accurate.

What types of items can I create?

Retail, prepared food & beverage, ingredient, and supply. Recipe configuration is available on prepared food & beverage items.

Related guide: Recipes & ingredients overview →

Do I manage items in Inventory or Menu Manager?

Either. Both use the same item data. Use Inventory for recipes and stock tracking, and continue managing menus in Menu Manager.

Related guide: Mastering the item catalog →

Do recipes need to exist before sales? Can I edit them later?

No. You can enter a manual plate cost and back-date it up to 30 days. You can also update recipes over time without creating new menu items.

Related guide: Recipes & ingredients overview →

Do I need to recreate recipes at every location?

No. A recipe follows its menu item to the locations where that item is shared. Editing the recipe updates it across those locations.

Related guide: Recipes & ingredients overview →

How do unit conversions work?

Conversions are stored on the ingredient and used wherever that ingredient appears. Check them carefully: incorrect conversions affect inventory calculations.

Related guide: Recipes & ingredients overview →

Can I edit multiple items at once?

Yes. Select items in the item catalog, then use the bulk action menus to update shared fields. Many fields can also be edited directly in the table.

Related guide: Mastering the item catalog →

Can I add my own fields to items?

Yes. Create custom attributes to store extra information, filter your catalog, and include values on labels.

Does this support location-specific pricing?

Yes. Reports and margins use each location’s price. Continue editing location-specific prices in your existing pricing tools.

Do recipe quantities change with modifier selections?

No. Recipes are set on the sellable item; checkout modifiers do not add or remove recipe ingredients.

Related guide: Recipes & ingredients overview →

Can I customize the allergen list?

No. You can tag finished items using the fixed, country-specific list. Allergens appear on POS and online ordering; third-party ordering is not supported.

Related guide: Recipes & ingredients overview →

Can I add custom dietary tags?

Yes. Dietary tags are customizable and appear on POS and online ordering. They are separate from allergen information.

Related guide: Recipes & ingredients overview →

Do ingredient allergens roll up to recipes?

No. Add allergens directly to the finished item.

Related guide: Recipes & ingredients overview →

Keep stock accurate

Stock counts & adjustments

Count what you have, record production, and correct stock.

How do I complete my first stock count?

Set up item locations, create a count template, enter the quantities you have, and submit the count.

Related guide: Completing your first stock count →

Why is my first count’s variance different from later counts?

Your first count sets the starting point. Later counts compare what you count with what Toast expects.

Related guide: Completing your first stock count →

Do I have to track stock on every item?

No. Choose which items to track. Counting an item automatically enables stock tracking for it.

Related guide: Completing your first stock count →

Can stock on hand go negative?

No. Stock on hand stops at zero until you count or receive more.

Related guide: Stock & historic corrections →

Can I back-date a receipt or correct an earlier stock entry?

Yes. Back-date or edit past stock entries to reflect when changes actually happened.

Related guide: Stock & historic corrections →

What recalculates when I edit a past stock entry?

Stock balances after the edited entry are recalculated throughout the subsequent history.

Related guide: Stock & historic corrections →

How do I add or disable adjustment reasons?

Go to Settings → Adjustment reasons. Add your own reasons or deactivate ones your team does not use.

Related guide: Adjustment reasons →

How do I record batch production?

Use stock counts or manual production events in the item catalog on the web or mobile app.

Related guide: Completing your first stock count →

Does POS waste update inventory?

Yes. POS waste appears in the item’s inventory activity. Configure waste reasons before logging waste.

Related guide: Real-time reporting →

Can I track stock balances by sub-location?

No. Separate stock balances by sub-location are not supported. Item locations organize your counts.

Related guide: Completing your first stock count →

Manage what sells

Sales availability & ordering

Keep availability aligned with stock and understand order timing.

When do I need to sync sales availability with stock?

Sales and POS waste update availability automatically. Sync after receiving inventory, negative Retail stock adjustments, or stock counts using Item actions → Sync availability with stock on-hand.

Related guide: Stock & sales availability →

Does changing quantity on the POS update stock on hand?

No. Changing quantity on a POS device updates sales availability. Counts, receipts, sales, and waste change stock on hand.

Related guide: Stock & sales availability →

Which ordering channels deplete inventory?

POS, online ordering, kiosk, and catering & events all deplete stock. For future orders, depletion happens when the order fires.

Related guide: Recipes & ingredients overview →

Can I set par levels?

Yes, for sellable items. Set par min and max in the item catalog. Ingredient and supply par levels, and automated restock-to-par, are not supported.

Related guide: Mastering the item catalog →

Do future orders reduce stock when they are placed?

No. Stock decreases when the order fires to the kitchen. Fire timing depends on your account’s settings.

Can I see stock committed to future orders?

No. Future orders do not reduce on-hand stock until they fire. There is no committed-stock view.

Do voids and refunds return stock?

A void before firing has no stock impact; a void after firing restocks the item. To refund without restocking, return the payment rather than voiding the order.

Understand your numbers

Costs, reporting & history

Check costs, current-day reports, and the detail behind them.

Which inventory reports are available?

Sales & COGS, Inventory Snapshot, and Inventory Roll Forward.

Related guide: Real-time reporting →

Do I need to wait until tomorrow to see today’s reports?

No. Reports include current-day activity without waiting for an overnight batch.

Related guide: Real-time reporting →

How do I check the detail behind a report number?

Open the item’s Inventory tab. Its activity log contains the individual movements behind summary report figures.

Related guide: Real-time reporting →

Does Sales & COGS include waste or stock adjustments?

No. COGS in this report focuses on sales, excluding waste, counts, and other stock movements.

Related guide: Real-time reporting →

Does COGS include items I do not track inventory for?

Yes. Sales & COGS includes all items that sell, including items without stock tracking.

Related guide: Real-time reporting →

Why do prices and costs appear for items with no sales?

Price and cost values display even when an item has not sold.

Related guide: Real-time reporting →

How is inventory cost calculated?

Using first-in, first-out (FIFO): the oldest received stock is consumed first. You can trace a sale’s cost to the receipt it used.

Related guide: Real-time reporting →

Can I see who changed an item and when?

Yes. Item history shows what changed, when, who made the change, and where it came from. Use Reports → History to review changes across your business.

Related guide: Item history →

Can I choose the number of decimal places?

No. Decimal precision is not customizable.

Can I ask questions about my inventory data?

Yes. Use Toast IQ to ask about stock levels and margin changes in plain language.

Integrations & moving from Toast Retail

Accounting, hardware, and the move from Toast Retail.

Which accounting options are supported?

QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop via IIF file, and export to Excel. QuickBooks Online supports sales, payroll, and accounts payable invoices. Accounting integrations are supported in the U.S. and Canada only.

Does this support scales or draft-system integrations?

No. Barcode scanning and label generation and printing are supported.

Can I generate barcodes and print labels?

Yes. Generate barcodes and customize and print labels individually or in bulk. Prep labels are not supported.

Related guide: Mastering the item catalog →

What happens to my existing Toast inventory product?

Most Toast Retail customers are expected to upgrade in January 2027; alternate-package exceptions are still being confirmed. For xtraCHEF or International Inventory, contact your Toast representative.

Related guide: Upgrading from Toast Retail →

What changes when I move from Toast Retail?

Sales availability is separate from stock. You also get real-time reporting, historic corrections, customizable adjustment reasons, and item history.

Related guide: Upgrading from Toast Retail →

When will Toast Retail customers upgrade?

We expect most retailers to upgrade in January 2027. Some alternate-package users may be exceptions; this is still being confirmed.

Related guide: Upgrading from Toast Retail →
