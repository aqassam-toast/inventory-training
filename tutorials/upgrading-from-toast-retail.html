Four things work differently in Toast IQ Inventory. Here's what's changing and what it means for you.

What's changing

Stock and sales availability now work independently.

Previously, one quantity controlled both what you had and what guests could buy. Now each item has two separate values, so you can keep selling even when stock on hand reaches zero.

What you gain

Keep selling

An out-of-date inventory no longer stops sales.

Preorders

Take orders on items whose stock hasn't come in yet.

Purchase limits

Cap sales at, say, 100, regardless of the stock number.

📱
This affects your POS devices
Updating an item's quantity on a POS device changes its sales availability, not your stock. Stock is only changed by counts, received orders, sales, and waste.
02

Every stock change is tracked in real time

Counts, received orders, sales, and waste show up in the activity log the moment they happen. Find it under the Inventory tab on each item page.

The activity log rolls up exactly to your Sales & COGS, Inventory Snapshot, and Inventory Roll Forward reports, so there's no more 24-hour delay on your critical reports.

03

Adjustment reasons that fit your business

Add the reasons your team actually uses and remove the ones you don't. Find them in Settings.

04

A clear history for every item

Reports show the detailed trail behind every quantity, not just the end result. Reporting reflects adjustments as soon as they happen, with no manual refresh needed.
