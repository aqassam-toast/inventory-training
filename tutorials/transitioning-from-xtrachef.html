xtraCHEF → Toast IQ Inventory

A new chapter.
With time to get there.

Moving your inventory workflows is a big step. We’ll give you time to plan, help from Toast, and a path that brings your essential data with you.

See the migration plan ↓
Time to make your move At least
until 2028

You’ll have at least until 2028 to upgrade. An early invitation doesn’t mean you need to move right away.

A phased rollout

An invitation when the fit is right.

We’ll phase invitations around the features you use in xtraCHEF and the capabilities available in Toast IQ Inventory.

  1. 1
    As early as Q1 2027

    First invitations

    We’ll begin inviting customers whose workflows are a good fit for the features available.

  2. 2
    Throughout 2027

    More customers, more capabilities

    Invitations will ramp up as Toast IQ Inventory’s features expand, based on what each customer uses today.

  3. 3
    At least until 2028

    Time to upgrade

    You’ll have time to prepare for your move, with Toast support during the migration.

Bring your foundation with you

Your work comes with you.

At a minimum, your migration will include the following—so you can build on the setup you already have.

xtraCHEF→Toast IQ Inventory

  • Products

    Your product catalog comes with you.

    ✓ Included in your move

  • Recent purchase data

    At least one quarter of recent purchase data will carry over.

    ✓ Included in your move

  • Recipes

    The recipes you’ve built will move with you.

    ✓ Included in your move

  • Mappings

    Your existing mappings will carry over.

    ✓ Included in your move

  • Unit conversions

    Your unit conversions will come across, too.

    ✓ Included in your move

Keep invoices for your records.

We’ll ensure you have a way to store your invoices for your records as part of the transition.

Toast is here to help

You won’t be making the move alone.

You’ll get Toast support during your migration to Toast IQ Inventory. Have questions about your workflows, data, or timing? We’re here to help you plan.
