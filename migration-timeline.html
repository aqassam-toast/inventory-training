Migration Timelines

How and when different customer cohorts will move to Toast IQ Inventory.

⚠️ Work in progress — do not communicate any details on this page to customers.

Feature Readiness
Product timeline

When capabilities become available in Toast IQ Inventory — separate from when existing customers will be able to migrate to them.

July 2026

Simple retail products

Stock counting, invoice scanning, and core purchasing & receiving.

September 2026 Now

Recipes & ingredients

Sales deplete ingredient inventory. Stock counts for ingredients. Lightweight purchasing, receiving, and import.

November 2026 Next

Shared inventory & bundles

Shared inventory (e.g. keg → pint), bundles, enhanced purchasing & receiving, and better reporting tie-in.

Migration of existing alternate packages and bundles may not be ready at this time. Timeline TBD.

December 2026

Modifier recipes

Inventory tracking for modifier-level recipes.

New Retail & International Restaurant Customers
September 2026

All new retail and international customers onboarded to Toast IQ Inventory

Starting September, new retail and international customers will be onboarded directly into Toast IQ Inventory rather than legacy inventory products.

Existing Retail Customers
June 30, 2026 Now

Opt in via banner

Eligible retailers can opt in to Toast IQ Inventory from a banner in the product.

November 23, 2026 Next

Forced opt-in for ~2,500 retail customers

About 2,500 retail customers are force opted in to the new experience.

January 1, 2027

Almost all retail customers move

Almost all remaining retail customers are forced into the new experience.

Potentially barring the migration of alternate packages. TBD.

Comms testing

Tease the new product to a small cohort of xtraCHEF customers to monitor interest and downsell. Scale if engagement is high and downsell is low.

Migration kickoff

Inform and align cross-functional stakeholders.

January – April 2027 Next

Migration pilot

Test the full designed migration journey, including self-service tooling and AMs/CSMs, with CS involvement.

Dependencies: modifiers; in-product migration tooling; EDI parity.

March 2027

Toast IQ Inventory sales GA

Sales stops selling xtraCHEF and starts selling Toast IQ Inventory across New Business and Upsell.

April 2027

Marketing GA

Promote Toast IQ Inventory at the Spring Release.

Through 2027

Migration continues…

Continue to migrate xtraCHEF customers throughout the year using a blend of self-service tooling, human-led support, proactive communications, customer education, etc.
