Beta Rollout Plan

How we're rolling out Toast Inventory to early customers — what access looks like, how to get started, and who's already in the beta.

TL;DR
Last updated August 14, 2026
  • This page now only covers production access. Interest in production access has grown, so we're no longer summarizing customers who only have sandbox access.
  • Most locations have retail products only. This covers stock counting, invoice scanning, and core purchasing & receiving.
  • Recipes & ingredients is being slow-rolled. Two customers have access right now, and more will have it by the end of the month.
  • Production access requires a request. New capabilities won't be enabled on production accounts automatically. Customers need to request access when ready.

How to get access

Request Toast Inventory through the access survey. List every account name you want it enabled on — the team will use those names to enable access on the correct accounts and send you a confirmation email.
